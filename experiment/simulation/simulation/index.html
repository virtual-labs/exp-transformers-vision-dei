1. Patch Extraction & Embedding

See how an image is split into fixed-size patches and flattened into vectors. This is the first step of the ViT pipeline.

Intuition: From Pixels to Vectors

The Vision Transformer (ViT) treats images like text. By chopping the image into fixed-size patches (e.g., 4x4 pixels), we convert 2D data into a 1D sequence of "tokens". Each flattened patch becomes a vector that represents a small part of the image, essentially creating a "sentence" where each word is a visual patch.

2. Attention Maps

Hover over patches to see what the model "looks at". Brighter colors indicate stronger attention weights.

Intuition: Self-Attention Mechanism

Self-Attention simulates "looking around". When we process a specific patch (green outline), the model computes attention scores to determine which other parts of the image are relevant. In the heatmap below, red areas indicate where the model is focusing its attention relative to the selected patch. Early layers often focus on nearby edges, while deeper layers find semantic connections (e.g., a "dog ear" attending to a "dog nose").

Deep Dive: Analyzing "Heads" & "Layers"

What is a "Head"?

An Attention Head is a specific search strategy.
• Head 0 (Local Context): Focuses on similar pixels nearby (texture).
• Head 1 (Global Similarity): Finds matching colors anywhere (e.g., blue sky).
• Head 2 (Proximity): Looks at immediate neighbors only.

What is a "Layer"?

Layers represent the level of abstraction.
• Early Layers (0-1): Constrained to local features. Even global heads search locally.
• Deep Layers (3+): Constraints lifted. The model searches the full image for semantic connections (e.g., nose ↔ tail).

3. Transformer Encoder

Trace the path of the patch embeddings through a Transformer Block.

Intuition: The Transformer Block

The Transformer Encoder is the brain of the network. The tokenized patches flow through layers of Multi-Head Attention (to mix information globally) and MLP (to process features locally).

Ultimately, a special "classification token" (or the average of all features) is used by the final MLP Head to determine the probability of each class.
